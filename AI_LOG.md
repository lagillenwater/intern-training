# AI-use log

Add one row every time you use AI help on this task. That includes Colab's built-in AI assistant and any chat tool. The rules are in the [AI use](README.md#ai-use) section of the task README.

What goes in each column:

- **Date**: the day you used AI, written like 2026-12-01.
- **Notebook**: the notebook number, such as 02.
- **What I asked**: your question, in a sentence or two.
- **What I kept**: the part of the answer you actually used.
- **What I changed or rejected**: what you changed or did not use, and why. "Nothing" is fine if it is true.

The first row is an example. You can leave it or delete it.

To add a row, open this file on GitHub while you are on your working branch, click the pencil icon, add a line at the bottom of the table, and commit the change to your working branch.
